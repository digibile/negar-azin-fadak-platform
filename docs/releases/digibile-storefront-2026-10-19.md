# Digibile storefront release — 2026-10-19

This release publishes the merged marketplace homepage improvements from PR #85.

- Normalize saved product fields before rendering.
- Make category filters tolerant of Persian/English spelling and spacing variants.
- Show saved products in mobile/digital, laptop/computer, home/kitchen, and watches/accessories sections.
- Show a special-offers section only for products with recorded discounts.
- Keep the product detail, favorites, and cart interactions already present in the storefront.
- Package the saved reference catalog with the release and verify the live domain after activation.

Discounts and prices must come from stored catalog data; do not invent product availability or discount values.
